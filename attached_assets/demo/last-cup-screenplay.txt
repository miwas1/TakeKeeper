THE LAST CUP

Written for the TakeKeeper demonstration

INT. KITCHEN — NIGHT

MAYA stands at the kitchen counter in an unzipped red jacket. A white mug sits
on the counter to camera right of her. A black phone rests immediately to the
right of the mug, with a set of keys farther right.

She studies the mug but does not touch it.

                         MAYA
             Some things are easier to notice
             after they change.

Maya looks toward the dark window.

                                                        CUT TO BLACK.
